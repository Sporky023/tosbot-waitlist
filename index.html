FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which platforms does TOSbot support?

X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube at launch, with more platforms on the roadmap. You can connect as many accounts as you like on a single subscription.

Can TOSbot post, edit, or delete things without me?

Never automatically. TOSbot is read-only by default. The "delete this post" button acts only when you tap it, and only where the platform's API allows deletion. Otherwise we deep-link you straight to the post so you can handle it yourself.

Does TOSbot read my DMs or private messages?

No. TOSbot only analyzes your own public posts and replies — the content platforms actually moderate for TOS enforcement. DMs are out of scope, full stop.

How accurate are the alerts?

TOSbot is tuned to be quiet. It flags posts that match the patterns platforms demonstrably enforce against — not every spicy opinion. You'll see the exact rule cited with each alert, so you can always judge for yourself. The final call is always yours.

Will this stop me from ever getting banned?

No tool can promise that — platforms make the final call, and their enforcement isn't fully predictable. What TOSbot does is close the biggest gap: risky posts sitting live for days because you didn't realize they were risky. Early awareness is your best defense.

Is TOSbot available today?

We're opening access in waves. If you want in, scroll to the bottom of this page and join the list — we'll email you when your spot opens. Free trial first, then about $4.99/month if you stick around.

What happens after the free trial?

14 days with features unlocked and no surprise charges. Near the end we'll ask if you want to continue around $4.99/month. If you don't, monitoring simply stops.

What do you do with my data?

We analyze your public posts to protect your account — that's it. We don't sell data, we don't build ad profiles, and you can disconnect an account or delete your TOSbot data at any time. See our privacy policy.
