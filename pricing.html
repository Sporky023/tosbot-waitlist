Pricing questions

Can I pay or start a trial today?

Access is opening in waves. Join the list at the bottom of this page and we'll notify you when your spot opens. No card required to get on the list.

Is there a limit on connected accounts?

One subscription covers all your personal accounts on supported platforms. Team and agency plans are on the roadmap — join the list at the bottom and tell us what you need.

How will canceling work?

Design goal: one tap in settings, effective at the end of the billing period. No retention gauntlet.

Is there an annual plan?

Not at first — we plan to launch monthly-only so you can judge TOSbot every month. An annual option with a discount is on the roadmap.
