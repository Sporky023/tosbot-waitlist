Privacy

Privacy Policy

TOSbot exists to protect your accounts — protecting your data is part of the same job. This is a plain-language placeholder policy; the full legal version will be published before launch.

The short version

We read your own public posts (read-only) to check them against platform rules. We alert you when something looks risky. That's the entire business. We don't sell your data, we don't build advertising profiles, and we don't read your private messages.

What we collect

  • Account basics: your email address and the platform accounts you choose to connect.
  • Your public posts: fetched via official platform APIs with read-only permissions, solely to analyze them for TOS risk.
  • Alert history: which posts were flagged and what action you took, so the app can show your history.

What we never collect

  • Direct messages or private content of any kind.
  • Your platform passwords — connections use official OAuth flows.
  • Data about people other than you.

What we do with it

Post content is analyzed to generate risk assessments and alerts for you. Flagged-post snippets are retained so you can review them in the app; unflagged post content is not retained after analysis.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting data to anyone.
  • No advertising or tracking pixels in the app.
  • No posting, editing, or deleting content without your explicit tap.

Your controls

  • Disconnect any platform account at any time.
  • Delete your TOSbot account and all associated data with one request — processed within 30 days.
  • Export your alert history on request.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@tosbot.app.
